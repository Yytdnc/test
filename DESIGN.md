# MindPick Design System

Modern, refined, minimal design system for MindPick psychological testing platform.

## Principles
1. **Clarity & Content-First**: Generous whitespace, high-contrast readable typography, subtle border lines instead of heavy drop shadows or flashy glows.
2. **Restrained Color Palette**: Deep charcoal / slate (#2c3e50) accent paired with warm neutral surfaces (#f8f8f8, #ffffff) and dark legible text (#1a1a1a).
3. **Tactile Interaction**: Subtle scale transforms (0.98) on click/active states and smooth elevation shifts rather than bouncy cartoon animations.
